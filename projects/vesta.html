Mission Overview

🪐 The Target: Vesta

Vesta is a differentiated asteroid and surviving protoplanet from the earliest era of Solar System history.

❓ The Question

Its ancient crust and interior record how rocky planetary bodies formed, differentiated, and evolved.

🚀 The Solution: Orbital survey + in-situ exploration + sample return

VISIT.TA combines orbital survey, in-situ exploration, and sample return for high-precision laboratory analysis.

Why Vesta Matters

🌋 A Surviving Protoplanet

Vesta preserves evidence from the building blocks of terrestrial planets.

🧭 A Window into Planet Differentiation

Its layered structure helps explain how early worlds separated into crust, mantle, and core.

🪨 The Origin of HED Meteorites

Returned samples can connect Vesta directly to meteorites studied on Earth.

Three Scientific Questions

  • Revealing the protoplanet interior
  • Understanding HED meteorites
  • Decoding giant impacts

Sample Analysis Workflow

Returned material moves through six lab stages, each tied back to one of the mission's three core scientific questions.

01

Contamination-free Unsealing

Return capsule opened in a nitrogen-purged glovebox to preserve pristine volatile and organic content.

Goal 1 · Protoplanet Interior
02

Curation & Cataloging

Sample split, weighed, imaged, and archived under strict planetary-protection protocols.

Goal 1 · Protoplanet Interior
03

Mineralogy & Petrology Analysis

Thin-section and XRD analysis to compare mineral assemblages directly against HED meteorite collections.

Goal 2 · HED Meteorites
04

Isotope & Chronology Dating

Radiometric dating constrains the timing of Vesta's crust-mantle-core differentiation.

Goal 1 · Protoplanet Interior
05

Volatile & Impact-Record Analysis

Shock features and volatile inventories reveal the history of major basin-forming impacts.

Goal 3 · Giant Impacts
06

Comparative Meteorite Cross-matching

Spectral and isotopic fingerprints are cross-matched to Earth's HED meteorite archive to confirm provenance.
